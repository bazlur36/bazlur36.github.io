Salah Jamat Times — Delete your account

This page is for the Salah Jamat Times app by Bazlur Rashid. You can delete your Salah Jamat Times account and its personal data at any time.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Salah Jamat Times and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) at the top right.
  3. Tap Profile (প্রোফাইল).
  4. Tap Delete Account (অ্যাকাউন্ট মুছুন) and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email mailtobazlur@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login (email and password, Google Sign-In or Facebook login)
  • Your name and email address

What is kept

Masjids, prayer time updates and janaza announcements you added remain available to the community but are no longer linked to your name or email. If you want specific content removed as well, say so in your email.
