Salah Jamat Times — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Salah Jamat Times ("the app") is developed by Bazlur Rashid. It shows nearby masjids, their jamat (congregational prayer) times, and janaza (funeral prayer) announcements. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

Your location

When you allow location access, the app uses your device's precise location to find masjids near you. Your coordinates are sent to our server only to look up nearby masjids and are not stored. You can use the app's "Search by Area" feature instead of sharing your location. The app only uses location while it is open.

Account information (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you create one — with email and password, Google Sign-In or Facebook login — we store:

  • your email address
  • your name (as you entered it, or as provided by your Google or Facebook account)

Content you add

Signed-in users can add masjids, update prayer times, post janaza announcements, and report problems with a masjid listing or a post. We store what you submit together with your account ID.

Janaza announcements can include details about the deceased person (such as name, father's name, age, relation, place of residence, cause and place of death). Only enter information the family is comfortable making public.

What other users can see

  • Masjids, prayer times and janaza announcements are public — anyone using the app can see them.
  • On a masjid's details page, the name of the person who added the masjid and of the person who last updated its prayer times is shown. Your email address is not shown to other users.
  • Problem reports about a masjid are visible to signed-in users. Reports about a post are seen only by us.

If you hide a person's posts, that choice is stored only on your phone.

How we use information

Only to run the app: finding nearby masjids, showing prayer and janaza times, signing you in, and attributing contributions. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the app contains no ads or analytics/tracking SDKs.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and server functions.
  • Google Sign-In (only if you choose it) authenticates you and shares your name and email with the app.
  • Facebook Login (only if you choose it) authenticates you and shares your name and email with the app.
  • Google Maps opens when you tap "directions"; that happens in the Maps app or website under Google's own privacy policy.

These providers process data on our behalf; we do not share your data with anyone else.

Security

All data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted by database access rules.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open the menu → Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, email and name immediately.
  • Without the app: see https://bazlur36.github.io/salah-jamat-times-legal/delete-account.html or email mailtobazlur@gmail.com from your account's email address; we will delete it within 30 days.

Masjids, prayer time updates and janaza announcements you contributed stay in the app because other people rely on them, but they are no longer linked to you. To have specific content removed as well, email mailtobazlur@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
